## Lookalikes, scams, and borrowed names

Search and chat are crowded:

- Lookalike domains with extra words or odd TLDs  
- “Agents” who register for you after you send OTP  
- APK gifts named Mod / Unlimited  
- Sure-shot colour channels on Telegram  
- Pages that drop the **E** or mix a near-name into a **6E Club** pitch  

**Check a link:** HTTPS, spelling of **6E Club**, no UPI PIN to “verify,” no pay-to-activate register.  
**If you already used a fake site:** change reused passwords, watch UPI, report **1930** / [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in). Full list: [scam checklist](/guides/scam-checklist).

## Safety and the law in one breath

Online **money** games in India sit under strict national rules (Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 — enforcement has been publicly discussed around 2026).
