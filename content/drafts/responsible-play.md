# Responsible Play

**18+ only.** Colour and number rounds move fast. Even practice coins can pull you into “one more period.” Money products are a separate, serious decision under Indian law.

**6eclubregister.com** does not take deposits and does not encourage real-money wagering.

## Before you start

- Set a **time** limit — use a phone timer.  
- If any product asks for payment, set a **money** limit first and stick to it.  
- Do not chase losses.  
- Keep at least one or two days a week completely off if you feel pulled back in.  
- Prefer slower rooms when learning — see [6E Club Game](/6e-club-game).

## Warning signs

- Playing instead of sleep or meals  
- Borrowing or hiding spend  
- Irritable when you try to stop  
- Believing a tip channel will “fix” losses  

If these show up, stop and talk to someone you trust.
