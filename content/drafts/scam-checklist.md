## Stop immediately if

Tick these. One yes = close the tab or uninstall.

- [ ] Pay ₹ to “activate,” “unlock,” or “verify” registration  
- [ ] Someone wants your **UPI PIN**, full card number, or CVV  
- [ ] “Support” asks you to read an **OTP** aloud or forward it  
- [ ] Aadhaar / PAN selfie through a random WhatsApp link  
- [ ] APK only from Drive / Telegram / “sir sending file”  
- [ ] Filename has **Mod / Hack / Gift / Unlimited**  
- [ ] App wants **SMS** or **Accessibility** for a simple game  
- [ ] AnyDesk / remote app to “fix login”  
- [ ] Guaranteed next colour for a fee  
- [ ] Domain drops the **E** or adds weird extra words you do not recognise

## Check a link in 30 seconds

1. Open in **Chrome or Safari**, not inside the chat WebView.  
2. Read the full hostname. One wrong letter is enough.  
3. Prefer a bookmark you saved after a good [register](/) / [login](/6e-club-login).  
4. HTTPS alone is not proof — fakes use HTTPS too.  
5. If the page screams “bonus expires in 2 minutes,” slow down on purpose.

**Bookmark once** on a calm day. Next time, open the bookmark — not a new sticker pack link.

## Fake 6E Club Login

Clones copy the login screen to steal passwords.

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|-------|------|------|
| Host | Matches the one from register | Almost right spelling |
| Fields | Mobile + password (± OTP) | UPI PIN to “verify login” |
| Source | Bookmark / trusted app | YouTube “customer care” link |

If you typed a password on a bad page: change it on the real host via forgot password, change it everywhere you reused it, watch UPI. Guide: [6E Club Login](/6e-club-login).

## Fake customer care numbers

Numbers under YouTube videos, Telegram bios, and comment spam are a classic drain.

They will ask for:

- OTP “to verify your UID”  
- Screen share / remote access  
- A small “unfreeze” or “GST” payment  
- A new APK “because your app is banned”

**Rule:** only use in-app / on-host help on the same site you registered. This guide site (**6eclubregister.com**) has no phone OTP desk.

## OTP and UPI traps

| Trap | What they want | Your move |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| “Forward OTP” | Your account / wallet | Never |
| Collect request you did not start | Money out | Decline + call bank |
| “Verify UPI with PIN” | PIN | Refuse |
| QR from a stranger in chat | Payment to them | Close chat |

OTP is cash on an Indian phone. Treat it that way.

## Sure-shot prediction scams

Colour rounds are chance. Paid “prediction experts,” VIP tip channels, and hack apps that “show the next colour” sell subscriptions — not certainty.

If play stops being fun, stop. Limits: [responsible play](/responsible-play). Free mental-health support in India includes Tele-MANAS **14416**.

## If you already used a fake site

1. Stop further payments — ignore “tax to release balance.”  
2. Uninstall unknown APKs; revoke install-unknown permission.  
3. Change passwords you typed (and anywhere reused).  
4. Open your UPI / bank app — decline strange collect requests; call the bank if PIN/OTP was shared.  
5. Keep screenshots of the fake URL, numbers, and UTR.  
6. Report (next section).  
7. Only then create a fresh account — on a host you typed yourself — via [6E Club Register](/).

## How to report in India

- Cyber fraud helpline: **1930**  
- National portal: [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in)  
- Bank / UPI app dispute channels for unauthorised payments  
- Report the chat, group, or listing inside WhatsApp / Telegram / YouTube  

Speed matters more than perfect essays. Call while the trail is fresh.

## Safe habits that actually work

- Bookmark the real host after a clean register/login.  
- Open chat links with **Open in Chrome/Safari**.  
- One person, one mobile number, one host.  
- No Mod APKs.  
- No OTP to strangers.  
- Time limit before long sessions — [responsible play](/responsible-play).  
- When unsure, read [register](/), [login](/6e-club-login), or [APK](/6e-club-apk) on this site — then decide.

## Frequently asked questions

No. Many are normal products or guides. The checklist is for the fakes mixed into the same searches and chats.
